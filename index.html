How it works

From model download to grounded answers, all on your phone.

  1. Load a model

    Download a compatible GGUF model from Hugging Face, or import one you already have.

  2. Run it locally

    llama.cpp runs the LLM directly on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

  3. Add documents

    Loki extracts and chunks the text from your PDF, DOCX and TXT files.

  4. Semantic search

    BGE-small creates embeddings, and ObjectBox vector search finds the most relevant passages.

  5. Grounded answers

    Retrieved context is combined with your prompt, so answers draw on your own documents.

  6. Agent mode

    For multi-step tasks, the model can call built-in tools such as a calculator and document search.
